## 1. Executive Verdict & Core Anti-Fraud Finding

Prior to the October 16, 2026 NSE session, the candidate pre-market model retraining pipeline was formally audited using out-of-sample forward observations gathered during the live session on October 15, 2026.

### Primary Forensic Finding:
When evaluated strictly against **true forward outcomes ($T \to T+1$ minute)** across all **8,262 verified predictions**, the active model ensemble achieved an out-of-sample directional accuracy of:
$$\mathbf{45.76\% \quad (\text{Sub-50\% — Statistically Indistinguishable from Random Noise / Inverted Edge})}$$

Furthermore, the model's Mean Absolute Error (MAE) was:
$$\mathbf{13.76\text{ bps vs. } 5.07\text{ bps for a Naive Zero-Return Baseline}}$$

Under the non-negotiable **Anti-Fraud and Capital Protection Rules**:
1. Any candidate model or existing model exhibiting sub-50% out-of-sample directional accuracy **must not be deployed to execute live orders or paper orders with simulated fills**.
2. Retraining on raw, unverified, or non-stationary intraday ticks without walk-forward cross-validation creates severe overfitting and negative drift.
3. **Verdict:** Candidate retraining is **REJECTED**. The model weights remain **frozen**, and runtime operations are restricted to **SHADOW MODE ONLY** (observation and prediction recording with zero order execution).

## 3. Investigation of the Previous "Tracking Illusion"

In previous reports, a chart titled `real_vs_predicted_chart.png` was presented which appeared to show predicted prices tracking actual prices almost perfectly.

### Root Cause Audit:
A line-by-line inspection of `chart_generator.py` revealed that the script plotted:
$$P_{\text{plotted\_prediction}}(t) = P_{\text{close}}(t) \cdot (1 + \hat{\alpha}_t)$$
directly alongside $P_{\text{close}}(t)$ at the **same timestamp $t$**.

Because the predicted alpha $\hat{\alpha}_t$ is small ($\pm 5$ to $15$ bps, or $0.05\%$), the plotted value $P_{\text{close}}(t) \cdot (1 + \hat{\alpha}_t)$ differs from $P_{\text{close}}(t)$ by only a fraction of a rupee (e.g. ₹0.40 on a ₹950 stock). When viewed on a full-day chart, the red line was visually identical to the blue line.

However, a genuine one-step-ahead forecast at time $t$ is a prediction of the price at **$t + 1$ minute**. When the prediction from time $t$ is aligned against the actual outcome at $t + 1$, the correlation collapses, and directional accuracy is revealed to be 45.76%.

### Anti-Fraud Remediation:
- `v3/reports/chart_generator.py` was rewritten to shift predicted targets forward to $T+1$.
- `test_05_chart_forward_alignment` was added to the regression suite to prevent regression to concurrent alignment.
- The chart generator now computes and plots prediction error in basis points explicitly.
