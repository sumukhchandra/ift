## Executive Summary

This diagnostic proves that the loss was **not** caused by a market flash crash or a stop-loss trigger. It was a classic **Chop Entry + Rigid Timeout Exit** failure mode:
1. **Entry into Low-Volume Chop:** The trade entered short when volume Z-score was **-1.15** and Micro-RSI was **6.7** (already deeply oversold) within a confirmed `SIDEWAYS` regime.

3. **Friction-to-Move Imbalance:** Transaction costs (₹6.58) exceeded gross market movement (₹5.40), accounting for **54.9% of the total net loss**.

### 8. Why did TIME_EXPIRATION_TIMEOUT occur?


Because the trade had not reached +3.5% profit, had not activated the trailing stop, and had not hit the -0.7% stop loss, it sat open until elapsed time exceeded 120 minutes.

The trade was essentially flat dead-money throughout its duration.

### 10. Was there a profitable exit opportunity?

### 11. Why wasn't the position closed earlier?
The engine completely lacked a **stagnation / time-decay exit rule**. If an intraday high-frequency trade fails to move in the predicted direction within 15 to 30 minutes, holding it for 2+ hours into low-volume chop exposes the capital to time decay and friction drag without any directional momentum.
